## Card bench protocol (adopted 2026-10-25, Q17-delta6 — standing laws for every card window)

1. Decode kernels are timed at verify widths (M up to draft-window+1), not M=1 alone.
2. Harnesses rotate weight copies past L2 (XTX 96 MB: a non-rotated bench of a ≤96 MB
   weight times L2, not GDDR6).
3. A/B comparisons are alternating runs (A,B,A,B), never two far-apart runs.
4. rocprofv3 on harnesses only, ≤4 counters, never against a live server.
5. Register pressure is censused with `tools/spill_census.py` (v2: sgpr spills,
   near-ceiling list, LDS list, private-w/o-spill flag) after any kernel change —
   a timing table only times what its fixture holds.
